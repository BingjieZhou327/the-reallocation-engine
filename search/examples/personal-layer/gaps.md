## Human review — rows removed from the AI draft

- **Killed G1 — Published, reviewable UX case-study evidence.** I already have a portfolio and normally provide it separately from my resume; the absence of its URL in a one-page resume does not justify concluding that the portfolio or case studies are missing. I should still ensure I share a working portfolio link with applications.
- **Removed G2 — Applied design-system evidence (relevance challenge).** The AI treated missing detail about Figma-related artifacts in my resume as a priority gap. I intentionally keep routine tool descriptions brief because Figma proficiency alone is not a competitive differentiator. **Important distinction:** this does *not* prove I have created a reusable design system; if a specific target posting requires that experience, I should verify it separately from my actual project artifacts rather than claim the requirement is already met.
- **Removed G4 — Post-research implementation follow-through not explicitly documented (evidence-format challenge).** My resume intentionally prioritizes concise descriptions of research contributions and the observed business outcomes rather than listing every intermediate cross-functional decision. Missing process detail in the resume does not establish that I failed to follow up on implementation. The reported 17.6% sales growth and 23.6% satisfaction improvement should still be described as outcomes my work **contributed to**, not as causal effects proven solely by the resume. Any stronger causal claim would need supporting documentation.

**Priority:** G3 is the most important gap for me because it reflects limited direct experience with structured variant comparison, not simply a choice about how to summarize work on a resume. G5 remains as an evidence gap relevant to design-oriented positions.
